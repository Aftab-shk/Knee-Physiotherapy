Exercise guide

See the move before you make it.

Every exercise in the programme, drawn from one moving figure. Drag the knee ceiling to see what a capped plan looks like: the figure bends only as far as the limit allows, and the knee marker turns amber, then red, exactly as the webcam tracker does. Squats are drawn face-on, because what matters there is where the knee points, not how far it bends.
